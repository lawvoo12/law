How the listings work

  • Every listing links to the regulator's public register — such as the SRA, the Law Society or the state bar — so you can check the lawyer or firm yourself before you hire them.
  • Names, office addresses and practice areas come from the firm's own website.
  • No paid rankings, and no made-up ratings or reviews.
  • Lawvoo is a directory, not a law firm, and doesn't give legal advice.
