About Wrongful Death Law

Wrongful Death Lawyer vs Fatal Accident Attorney — What You Need to Know

Whether you call it a wrongful death claim, fatal accident lawsuit, death by negligence case, or wrongful death suit — when a loved one dies due to another person's negligence or intentional act, the family has the right to seek financial compensation.

Wrongful death claims can be filed by the spouse, children, parents, or other dependents of the deceased. Compensation can include funeral expenses, lost income, loss of companionship, emotional suffering, and in some cases punitive damages.

Wrongful Death vs Fatal Accident Claim

A wrongful death claim is a civil lawsuit filed when someone dies due to another party's negligence — car accidents, medical malpractice, workplace accidents, defective products. A fatal accident attorney handles these complex cases and fights insurance companies and corporations for maximum compensation for your family.

FAQ

Wrongful Death Lawyer — Fatal Accident & Death by Negligence Attorney USA FAQ

Everything you need to know about Wrongful Death claims in the USA.

Who can file a wrongful death lawsuit?
Typically the spouse, children, or parents of the deceased can file. Some states allow siblings, grandparents, or financial dependents. A wrongful death attorney can confirm who is eligible in your specific state.
How much is a wrongful death case worth?
Wrongful death settlements average $500,000 to $1 million or more, depending on the deceased's age, income, the circumstances of death, and the number of dependents. Fatal accident cases involving children or breadwinners often result in very high awards.
What damages can I recover in a wrongful death case?
You can recover funeral and burial expenses, medical bills from the final injury, lost income and future earnings, loss of companionship, emotional suffering, and in cases of gross negligence, punitive damages.
How long do I have to file a wrongful death claim?
The statute of limitations for wrongful death varies by state — typically 2 years from the date of death. Some states have shorter deadlines. Contact a wrongful death attorney immediately to protect your family's rights.
Is a wrongful death case different from a criminal case?
Yes — a wrongful death lawsuit is a civil case filed by the family for financial compensation. A criminal case is filed by the government for punishment. Both can proceed simultaneously — a not guilty verdict in criminal court does NOT prevent a wrongful death civil lawsuit from succeeding.
