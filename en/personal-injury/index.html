About Personal Injury Law

What is a Personal Injury Lawyer and What Do They Do?

A personal injury lawyer, injury attorney, or accident lawyer represents people who have been injured due to someone else's negligence or intentional actions. Whether you were in a car accident, bitten by a dog, injured in a slip and fall, or harmed by medical malpractice — a personal injury attorney fights for your right to compensation.

Personal injury attorneys work on a contingency fee basis — meaning they only get paid if you win. You pay nothing upfront. The typical fee is 33% of the settlement amount. This means anyone — regardless of financial situation — can access quality legal representation.

Personal Injury Attorney vs Accident Lawyer vs Injury Claim Lawyer

These terms all mean the same thing — a lawyer who specializes in cases where someone was injured due to another party's negligence. Whether you search for a personal injury lawyer, accident attorney, injury claim lawyer, or no win no fee attorney — our verified attorneys handle all types of injury cases across all 50 US states.

Steps to Take

What to Do After a Personal Injury Incident

Critical steps to protect your health and your legal rights.

1

Seek Medical Help

Get medical attention immediately — even if you feel fine. Medical records are critical evidence in your case.

2

Report the Incident

Call 911. File an official report. Create an immediate paper trail that supports your claim.

3

Document Everything

Photos of scene, injuries, damages. Witness contact info. Keep all medical records and receipts.

4

Don't Admit Fault

Never say sorry or admit fault — let the investigation determine liability.

5

Avoid Insurance Traps

Do NOT accept any settlement without consulting a lawyer. Initial offers are always too low.

FAQ

Personal Injury Lawyer — Find Top Injury Attorneys USA FAQ

Everything you need to know about Personal Injury claims in the USA.

What is a personal injury lawyer?
A personal injury lawyer — also called an accident attorney, injury attorney, or personal injury attorney — represents people injured due to someone else's negligence. They handle cases involving car accidents, slip and falls, dog bites, medical malpractice, wrongful death, and more.
How much does a personal injury lawyer cost?
Most personal injury lawyers work on contingency — they only get paid if you win. The typical fee is 33% of the settlement. You pay nothing upfront and nothing if you lose. This is called a 'no win no fee' arrangement.
How long do I have to file a personal injury claim?
The statute of limitations varies by state — typically 2 to 3 years from the date of injury. Missing this deadline means losing your right to compensation forever. Contact a personal injury attorney immediately.
What compensation can I receive?
You may be entitled to medical expenses, lost wages, loss of earning capacity, property damage, pain and suffering, emotional distress, and in cases of gross negligence, punitive damages.
Should I accept the insurance company's offer?
No — never accept the first offer without consulting a personal injury lawyer. Insurance companies are legally obligated to minimize payouts. Injury attorneys routinely negotiate settlements 3-10x higher than initial offers.
Do I need a lawyer for a personal injury case?
While not required, having a personal injury attorney significantly increases your chances of fair compensation. Studies show injury victims with lawyers receive on average 3.5x more compensation than those without.
