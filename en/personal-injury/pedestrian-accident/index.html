About Pedestrian Accident Law

Pedestrian Accident Lawyer vs Hit by Car Attorney — What You Need to Know

Whether you call it a pedestrian accident, hit by car, struck by vehicle, crosswalk injury, or pedestrian vs car accident — if you were injured while walking due to a driver's negligence, you have the right to seek maximum compensation.

Pedestrians are the most vulnerable road users. When a car strikes a person on foot, the injuries are almost always severe — broken bones, traumatic brain injury, spinal cord damage, internal injuries, and in many cases death. Drivers have a duty to yield to pedestrians — when they fail, they are liable.

FAQ

Pedestrian Accident Lawyer — Hit by Car & Crosswalk Injury Attorney USA FAQ

Everything you need to know about Pedestrian Accident claims in the USA.

What is the difference between a pedestrian accident lawyer and a hit by car attorney?
They are the same — pedestrian accident lawyer, hit by car attorney, crosswalk injury lawyer, and struck by vehicle attorney all specialize in cases where a person on foot was injured by a vehicle.
Can I recover compensation if I was jaywalking when I was hit?
Possibly — even if you were jaywalking, you may still recover compensation depending on your state's fault laws. Most states use comparative negligence, meaning your award is reduced by your percentage of fault. Contact a pedestrian accident attorney to understand your rights.
How much is a pedestrian accident case worth?
Pedestrian accident settlements are typically higher than car accident cases because injuries are usually more severe. Average settlements range from $50,000 to $250,000+. Severe TBI, spinal cord, or wrongful death cases can result in much higher awards.
What if the driver who hit me fled the scene?
You may still recover compensation through your own uninsured motorist (UM) coverage or through a hit-and-run fund if one exists in your state. A pedestrian accident attorney can help you explore all available options.
How long do I have to file a pedestrian accident claim?
The statute of limitations is typically 2-3 years from the date of the accident. Contact a pedestrian accident lawyer immediately to preserve evidence — surveillance footage is often deleted within days.
